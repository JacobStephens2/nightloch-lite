# Bound Partner Activity by the Partnership lifetime

Partners need Activity to verify changes affecting a Protectee, but retroactive or continuing access would disclose information from outside their relationship. A Partner therefore sees Activity beginning with their acceptance and loses ongoing Activity and Roster access when the Partnership ends; afterward they retain only a read-only receipt for their own ended Partnership. A later Partnership between the same Users gets a new visibility window and does not reveal the gap. Before acceptance, the invite landing page withholds existing Partners’ identities. If the Protectee deletes their User, a former Partner’s receipt survives only as **Deleted User**, the deletion ending cause, and exact time; retained entries likewise pseudonymize a deleted actor rather than erasing attributable actions from another Protectee’s history.
